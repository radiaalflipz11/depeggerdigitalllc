05 — FAQ

Questions.

What file formats can you deliver?

Standard formats such as MP4 and MOV, plus platform-specific exports on request.

Do you edit in English and Thai?

Yes. We add English and Thai subtitles and can edit footage in either language.

How do revisions work?

Each package includes the feedback rounds listed on the plan. Extra rounds are quoted separately.

How do I send my footage?

Send a download link from Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer, along with your brief and deadline.

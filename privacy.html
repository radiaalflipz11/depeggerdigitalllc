Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Depegger Digital LLC ("Depegger Digital", "we", "us") is a video editing studio registered in New Mexico, USA. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and in the course of our work, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you email or call us, such as your name, email address, phone number and company.
  • Project materials you send us for editing, such as video footage, audio, images, scripts and briefs. These may contain personal information about you or other people who appear in the material.
  • Billing details needed to issue quotes and invoices and to receive payment. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Website usage: this site does not use analytics cookies. It stores one preference in your browser (your chosen language) and loads fonts from Google Fonts and video thumbnails and players from YouTube, which may set their own cookies under their own policies.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries, prepare quotes and deliver the editing work you ask for.
  • To invoice you and keep the accounting and tax records we are required to keep.
  • To communicate with you about your project, including drafts and feedback.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use your project materials for any purpose other than your project, unless you give us written permission (for example, to show the finished work in our portfolio).

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as email, cloud storage, file transfer and payment providers, and only as needed to do the work. We may also disclose information when required by law.

4. Retention

We keep project materials for as long as needed to complete the project and handle revisions, and then delete them unless you ask us to keep them or we need them to resolve a dispute. Invoices and accounting records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect the information we hold, including access controls on our storage accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop processing it. Email us at admin@depeggerdigitalllc.store and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
